Software Engineering Group Code of Conduct

The purpose of this code of conduct is to make sure that our group is working fairly, meets our deadlines, communicates well and that we make it through the group project without falling out over GitHub.
Communicate
Everyone should be regularly checking the group chat and responding to important messages within a reasonable time frame
If you cannot attend class, a planned meeting or group working session, let your team know as early as possible
If something confuses you, ask the team. We’re all here to help each other, and it's so much easier to ask than to stress and worry on your own for for days hoping a miracle happens
Any important decisions made by the group for group work should be well communicated to the entire group so nobody can accidentally be left out
Attendance and Meetings
Everyone should make or try to make a reasonable effort to attend any meetings scheduled and the relevant lectures/labs
Meetings should start and end roughly as agreed unless decided at the meeting to finish early
If you're going to be late, it is only fair and reasonable to send a message to the group chat to inform them
Missing a meeting without letting the team know could result in
Buying the group snacks for the next meeting
Doing a lap around the building or up and down the stairs to the top floor and back
Getting temporarily demoted to junior developer (not getting your favourite preferred job)
Exceptions for illness, emergencies or genuine circumstances because we know things happen outside our control
Workload
Work will be divided as fairly and evenly as possible based on each member's skills, availability and what the project requires
If you agree to a task, it's your responsibility to do it (of course, get help where and if it's needed)
If you notice you can't finish something before the agreed deadline, communicate this with the group before the deadline (not five minutes before it's due)
No single member will be left to carry the entire project.
Helping each other is always encouraged; doing all the work and not learning how to do it is not.
Deadlines
Internal deadlines should hopefully be set before university deadlines so we can edit and fix problems together.
Work should be uploaded and shared between the group with time for everyone to review it.
“It works fine on my laptop/ at home” is acceptable once; we will require evidence if this is repeated
Leaving your entire section until submission day might result in public judgement from the group
Github/Code management
Commit your changes earlier rather than uploading half a project in one huge commit
Make your commit messages meaningful: “added log in validation” not: “stuff” “idk” “version 3”
Make sure you pull the latest version before major changes are made.
Don’t overwrite or delete someone else's work without discussing it first.
Break the main branch and you may be required to formally apologise to GitHub
Quality of work
Everyone sjopuld aim to contriute work that’s completed to a standard that they would also be happy to submit personally under their own name
Code should be appropriately commented on and readable
Testing properly should always be done, running it once and calling it complete does not count
AI tools should only be used in alignment with Napiers rules and should not be replacing the understanding of the work
Respect
Everyones ideas are valid therefore should be listened to and considered
Feedback and criticism are to focus on improvements not on attacking a member of the tgroup
Disagreements on work should be communicated and discussed calmly as a group to resolve them
Nobody will be allowed to make another member fell stupid for asking for help
We are allowed to judge the code just maybe not the developer
Decision making
Where possible decisions will be made fairl as a group
If the group cant agree, majority votes will come into play
Acounatbilty
If someone repeatedly fails to complete work before deadlines that they agreed to
The group will discuss the issue with them
Responsibilities and deadlines will be clarified
If the problem repeatedly occurs, the group may have to inform a lecturer/module leader
The objective here is to solve the issues early rather than letting frustrstion build up until the point of submissions being due.
Group Rules
No-show without warnings: you owe the group ice cream
Late without warning: one lap around the block
Break the main branch: formal apology to the group and GitHub
Saying “ill do it later” three times in a row requires an actual deadline to be set
Suggest a meeting before 9am: have good justification for it
Final rule
We are all working towards the same goal. Communicate to the group. Do your part, help each other out, and don’t let a group project ruin friendships built along the way
By working as part of this group, members agree to make a reasonable effort to follow this Code of Conduct throughout the project.
